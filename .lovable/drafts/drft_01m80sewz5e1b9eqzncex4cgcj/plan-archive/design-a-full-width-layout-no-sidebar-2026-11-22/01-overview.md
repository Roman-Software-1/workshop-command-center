# Design A: full-width layout, no sidebar

Remove the lights sidebar and let everything use the full 1024x600 screen.

## Changes
- **Remove the lights sidebar** (switch, ON/OFF, lamp bank) from this draft.
- **Top section spans the full width:** Hours worked (biggest number, break time under it), clock + date + status, and the three clock buttons in one wide band across the top.
- **Middle:** weekly hours graph gets much wider; monthly heatmap sits to its right, slightly larger squares.
- **Bottom:** stats row (Tasks, Focus time, Focus score, top category) stretched full width, Spotify player under it, reaching the bottom edge.
- Everything still fits exactly 1024x600, no scrolling, status border and frosted flash unchanged.
