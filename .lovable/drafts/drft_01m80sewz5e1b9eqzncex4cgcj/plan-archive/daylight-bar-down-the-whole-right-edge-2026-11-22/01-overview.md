# Daylight bar down the whole right edge

Move the daylight bar out of the November block and make it a **full-height strip on the far right of the screen**, running from top to bottom.

- **The strip is about 84px wide and set off from the rest of the dashboard by one divider line.** The "9 HRS 50 MINS / DAYLIGHT" header sits at the top, the segmented bar fills the full height below it, and the sun and moon icons sit at the ends.
- The bar gets much taller (about 520px instead of 150px), so the colored blocks, hour marks (00 through 24), and the "now" arrow are easier to read from across the shop.
- Everything else (hours, clock, buttons, graph, heatmap, stats, Spotify) narrows slightly to make room. The heatmap goes back to its own block with no bar inside.
- The A/B/C design switcher moves to the top-left corner so it doesn't cover the daylight header.
- The screen stays at exactly 1024×600.
