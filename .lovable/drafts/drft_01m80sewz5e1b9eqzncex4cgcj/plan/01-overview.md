# Red-to-green month heatmap

Recolor the November squares so each day shows how much you worked:

- **0h (day off):** dim gray square
- **Under 3h:** red
- **3–5h:** orange
- **5–7h:** yellow
- **7–8h:** light green
- **8h+ (goal hit):** bright green

Future days stay as empty outlined squares. The "Less → More" key at the bottom uses the same five colors, red through green. Nothing else changes, and the screen stays at 1024×600.
