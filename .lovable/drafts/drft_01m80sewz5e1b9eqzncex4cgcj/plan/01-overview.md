# Week and month hours under "Hours worked"

Add two small totals directly under today's big hours number, on the same line as Break, so all your hours sit in one place at the top:

**Break 0h 35m · Week 37.8h · Month 70h**

- **Week** = total hours for the current week, Monday through today (sample data for now).
- **Month** = total for November so far (same number the heatmap shows).
- Same small size as Break, so today's hours stay the biggest number. Break stays orange, and the two totals use the normal text color.
- Nothing else moves, and the screen stays at exactly 1024×600.
